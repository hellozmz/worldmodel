RSSM notebook

Dreamer 的关键思想

不要在像素上直接学习全部动态，而是学习一个紧凑 latent state，再在这个 latent 空间里想象未来并训练策略。

posterior: q(z_t | o_t, h_t)
prior:     p(z_t | h_t)
recurrent: h_t = GRU(z_t, a_t, h_t)
decode:    z_t → o_t, r_t, done_t
loss:      reconstruction + reward + done + KL
